Key design decisions baked in:
Income is always step 1 — nothing else unlocks until it's set
Progress banner shrinks and disappears once all 4 steps are done
Each section shows example items (Netflix, Rent...) so users know what belongs there
Free users see everything — no paywalled sections on this page
"Up next" highlight on the current incomplete step
All sections stay visible and editable even after setup is complete
